buyingGuide:
  - heading: "Measure the drawer, not the counter"
    text: "Measure the inside width, depth and height of the drawer (not the drawer front). Expandable trays list a minimum and maximum width — your drawer should sit comfortably inside that range. Also check the height: a tray that's too tall will catch on the cabinet frame."
  - heading: "Expandable vs. fixed"
    text: "**Expandable** trays fill the whole drawer, so nothing slides around — the best choice for most people. **Fixed** trays have more structured compartments and work well in wide or deep drawers where you want dedicated spots for bulky tools."
  - heading: "Bamboo vs. plastic"
    text: "Bamboo looks warmer and is sturdy, but should be hand-washed and dried. Plastic is lighter, cheaper and easier to wipe down — a practical pick for families or the utensil drawer next to the sink."
