Garages collect everything that doesn't have a home somewhere else — holiday decorations, camping gear, tools, sports equipment. Good lidded bins keep it dry, dust-free and stackable, so you can actually find things again.

We compared popular garage storage bins with lids on Amazon by price per bin, capacity, rating and review volume.

The three picks below cover the three questions that actually drive this decision: how much capacity you need per dollar, whether brand reputation and review history matter more to you than price, and whether being able to see inside a bin without opening it is worth a bit more money. None of them is wrong — it comes down to what you're storing and how often you'll need to get back into a given bin.

If you're just starting to organize a garage, it's worth sorting everything into rough categories first — seasonal, rarely used, and frequently used — before buying any bins at all. That sorting pass usually reveals how many bins you actually need and which size makes sense, instead of guessing and ending up with too many of one size.

It's also worth resisting the urge to buy the single biggest bin available for everything. A garage full of 27-gallon totes looks efficient on paper, but a bin that heavy when full is awkward to lift off a high shelf and easy to put off dealing with. Mixing in a smaller, lighter size for things you access more often tends to make the whole system easier to actually use.
